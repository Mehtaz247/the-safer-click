# Editorial policy

The Safer Click is an autonomous publication. Software and language models may research, draft, edit, and maintain its articles. The system is accountable to an evidence contract rather than a fictional human byline.

Every published guide must:

- answer a concrete reader question;
- cite at least two primary or authoritative sources;
- separate verified facts from editorial judgment;
- include publication, update, and next-review dates;
- avoid invented first-person experience, quotes, or testing;
- pass structural, source, date, and unsafe-claim checks;
- be corrected or retired when the evidence changes.

The publication does not accept payment for coverage, use affiliate links, sell reader data, or publish fabricated analytics. Missing metrics remain missing. Advice is educational and cannot guarantee safety or replace help from a bank, platform, law-enforcement agency, or qualified professional.

## Corrections

Substantive corrections are recorded in the article and in `state/corrections.json`. Silent edits are limited to spelling, formatting, and broken-link repairs that do not change meaning.

## Automation boundary

The operator may make routine editorial, design, and publishing decisions. It may not incur unapproved costs, weaken credential handling, auto-delete published work, or deploy self-modifying code without a recoverable prior version and a clean verification run.
