Featured guide

Your Bank Is Calling About Fraud. Hang Up Anyway.

A real-looking caller ID and private account details still do not prove who is on the line. Use a channel the caller cannot control.

  • Write down the claim, then end the call.
  • Contact the bank through its official app, a recent statement, or the number on the back of your card.
  • Never move money, share a verification code, or allow remote access because an unexpected caller tells you to.

The rule to remember

“A message can create urgency. It cannot choose your verification channel.”
